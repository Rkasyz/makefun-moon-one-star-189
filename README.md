## The entry (110 characters)

> One star. Rises late, floods my bedroom, then vanishes for a week like it owes me rent. Would not orbit again.

110 characters, one line, inside the gate of 1–120 characters. The review
reads like a genuine one-star complaint: a rating, then three Moon-only
grievances — it rises late, floods the bedroom with light, and vanishes
for a week — with the last one landing the joke: the Moon disappears like
a tenant who owes rent. "Would not orbit again" closes it in the voice
of a reviewer who will not be coming back.
